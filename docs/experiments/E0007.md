# E0007 — Classroom sampling independence audit (blocking finding)

Date: 2026-11-11; Blender 5.0.1, Cycles CPU, official Blender Classroom CC0.

## Motivation
The proposed emission-preservation study required four independently noisy inputs and a reference with a truly different sample sequence. It is not enough for a metadata field to say the seed was changed.

## Independent process probe
External Classroom scene, exact same frame/camera/geometry, 96x54 pixels, 16 spp, with Blender launched separately for every variant.

| Changed property | Compared variants | Observed difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cycles seed, Classic/TABULATED_SOBOL | 539 vs 911 | RGB RMSE about 1.04e-6; only 4/5184 pixels altered |
| sample_offset, Classic/TABULATED_SOBOL | offset 0 vs 16, seed 539 | RGB RMSE about 1.04e-6; only 8/5184 pixels altered |
| Sampling pattern | Classic vs BLUE_NOISE | RGB RMSE 0.168249; clearly different output |
| Cycles seed, BLUE_NOISE | 539 vs 911 | RGB RMSE about 5.38e-10; effectively identical |

Evidence:
- https://github.com/mxlzz/ACD/actions/runs/43069937939 (Classic seed and offset)
- https://github.com/mxlzz/ACD/actions/runs/43071018867 (Blue Noise and two seed values)
- Output artifacts include unfiltered EXRs, metadata and comparison JSON.

## Consequences
The tested nominal seed labels do **not** produce independent noise
realizations. This finding holds for the specific Classroom scene and
Blender build tested; we do not yet know whether it is a renderer bug,
a scene configuration interaction, or expected behavior of the particular
sampling setup.

- Do not regard Classroom seed-loop experiments as independent trials.
- Do not report their means/rankings as multi-seed evidence.
- The single noisy render comparisons E0005/E0006 still quantify
  differences between denoising branches on the *same* pixels, but
  their finite-spp reference is not independent in the required sense.
- A second reference with a different spp budget but effectively
  identical sample sequence is NOT an independent Monte Carlo estimate.

## Controlled procedural scene (same Blender build)

The separate procedural interior fixture, with identical geometry, camera,
lights, resolution (96x54), 16 spp and two independently started Blender
processes, **did** produce distinct raw images for seeds 539 and 911.
Pairwise RGB RMSE divided by signal RMS: **0.26552**.

Evidence: https://github.com/mxlzz/ACD/actions/runs/43071534950

This is positive evidence that the renderer and detector can produce and
recognize genuinely different noisy inputs. The Classroom anomaly is
scene-specific in the tested configuration (exact root cause still unknown).

## Implemented safeguards
- acd_lab.seed_audit.verify_sample_variation requires a minimum RGB
  difference relative to signal RMS (diagnostic only).
- acd_lab.seed_audit.audit_noisy_seed_diversity now checks every pair,
  not only every seed against baseline.
- acd_lab.aggregate.aggregate_seed_reports labels unverified results
  and suppresses mean rank and win-count claims when independence
  screening cannot pass. Numeric aggregates remain accessible for
  debugging and carry a warning.
- New unit tests cover duplicates among extra seeds and distinct
  EXR input acceptance.

## Next experiment, in order
1. Check a known noisy **procedural Cycles** fixture at multiple
   seeds and verify images differ while geometry and lighting remain fixed.
2. Diagnose why Classroom ignores seed/offset, including render view-layer
   settings, animated seed and sampling configuration.
3. Verify input/reference sample diversity, not just raw-input pairs.
4. Return to emission-preservation validation only after independently
   varying noise is demonstrated on at least two scenes and multiple seeds.

Do not modify the public addon on the basis of unvalidated averaging.
