## Predeclared measures

Main: global scene-linear RGB RMSE; reference-selected edge-gradient RMSE (asinh RGB; edge mask only from first reference), flat gradient RMSE, HDR MAE, plus second-reference divergence. The two 512 spp references are diagnostic, not presumed converged. Ranking invalidated if input-seed diversity heuristic fails. Secondary: crops 1:1 of fine textures and reflections require increasing output resolution, as 320 px can alias true texture. Some texture/color errors at fixed low resolution may not reflect architectural production.

A quality claim requires repeated, more-converged references and a held-out *different scene*. No candidate should be optimized against Cozy Kitchen and then declared proven on Cozy Kitchen.
